⚠ Strata Operational Note — After-Hours AC Policy ("Lift Nyala, AC Koridor Mati")
Outside standard building hours (weekdays ~18:00–19:00, weekends), corridor air conditioning is shut off while lifts remain operational. This applies to both Office Tower 2 and DBS Tower as they share the same CW1 building management. Overtime AC for corridor and common areas is charged at a calculated additional cost per request — not included in the standard SC. Strata owners must define overtime AC cost allocation (owner vs. tenant) explicitly in the lease agreement before handover. Recommended structure: pass-through to tenant, payable directly to building management.

⬡ Complex & Commercial Connectivity

CW1 · Direct Podium Connection (shared with DBS Tower, same physical structure)
DBS Bank Tower — the other office tower in CW1. Both towers sit on the same podium and share Lotte Mall, Raffles Hotel, and Artpreneur access. From the perspective of a CW1 OT2 tenant, DBS Tower is an adjacent building on the same campus.
Lotte Mall (formerly Lotte Shopping Avenue) — 140,000 sqm premium retail & F&B mall at podium level. Direct connection from Office Tower 2 lobby without exiting the building.
Raffles Hotel Jakarta — 5-star, 200 keys. Shared podium access. Premier venue for client meetings and visiting investor delegations.
Raffles Residences Jakarta — 80 ultra-luxury branded units on the same podium. Adds ultra-high-net-worth residential catchment to the building's footfall.
Ciputra Artpreneur Center — 1,157-seat international theatre, gallery & museum. Jakarta's premier CBD cultural venue; unique brand asset shared by the whole CW1 block.
Ascott Kuningan / Somerset Grand Citra — serviced apartments connected via podium. International corporate long-stay tenants; built-in captive workforce on-site.
CW2 · Adjacent Integrated Block (same Jl. Satrio master development)
Tokopedia Tower — Grade A office (48F, GFA ~70,000 sqm), anchor tenant GoTo/Tokopedia. Elevates the address profile of the entire Jl. Satrio block for Grade A tenants.
W Hotels Jakarta — luxury lifestyle hotel; key F&B and entertainment destination serving the CW1–CW2 corridor.
The Newton 1 & Newton 2 — twin residential towers, 1,000+ units combined. Largest captive residential population on Jl. Satrio; strongest built-in workforce pool for both CW1 office towers.
The Orchard Satrio — premium condominium with mixed F&B podium. Adds retail footfall and residential density to the master block.
Fraser Suites + Ascott Sudirman Jakarta — international serviced apartments (192 units). Corporate expatriate long-stay; steady hospitality and F&B contribution to the block economy.

Section 03

⬡ Streets, Area & Transport Connectivity

Address & Street Context
Primary address: Jl. Prof. Dr. Satrio Kav. 3-5, Karet Kuningan, Kec. Setiabudi, Jakarta Selatan 12940 — identical to DBS Tower; both towers share the same site address on the CW1 block.
Main frontage: Jl. Prof. Dr. Satrio — major east-west connector from Sudirman (west) through Kuningan to Rasuna Said (east). Transjakarta Halte DBS Tower / CW1 sits directly fronting this side, serving both office towers.
Southern boundary: Jl. Gatot Subroto — major north-south arterial; Transjakarta Gatot Subroto corridor stops accessible from this side. Connects to Semanggi interchange and toll network.
Eastern axis: Jl. H.R. Rasuna Said — Kuningan and embassy corridor begins at this intersection. Building is at the western convergence, optimally positioned between the Sudirman and Kuningan axes.
Area context: Mega Kuningan sub-district, between Jl. Gatot Subroto (south) and Jl. Satrio (north). CW1 Office Tower 2 and DBS Tower together constitute the dominant Grade A office presence at this node — the closest point in Kuningan to the MRT network.
Administrative area: Karet Kuningan, Setiabudi, Jakarta Selatan — South Jakarta ✓ per client brief.
